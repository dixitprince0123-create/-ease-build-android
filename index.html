Common questions

Good to know.

Can I get a customised house naksha?

Yes, use the enquiry form to share your plot dimensions, rooms, floors, location and requirements. A professional review is required before construction.

Are material prices and labour available live?

Not yet. This demo website accepts enquiries; live supplier inventory, rates and worker availability require business onboarding and system integration.

Does the calculator give a final construction price?

No. It uses illustrative assumptions to demonstrate the calculator. A final quote requires verified rates, drawings, specifications and site details.

Can I pay online?

Online payments are not activated in this prototype. A verified merchant account, payment gateway integration, policies and security testing are required before accepting payments.
